Free Lead Generation Tools for Sales and Marketing Teams

Our Lead Gen & Scraping Scripts category brings together 6 browser-based tools that help you build and clean contact lists efficiently. Whether you need to extract email addresses from text blocks and content, extract phone numbers from strings, validate email address format and domain syntax, remove duplicates and format lead contact lists, build simple web scraping rules and patterns, or generate HTML contact form code with validation — these tools streamline your lead generation workflow.
