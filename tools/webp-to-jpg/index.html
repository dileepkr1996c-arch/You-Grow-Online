Why Convert WebP to JPG? A Complete Guide for Web Developers and Marketers

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior compression compared to JPEG and PNG. WebP images are typically 25-35% smaller than their JPEG counterparts at equivalent quality, making them excellent for website performance. However, WebP is not universally supported across all platforms, browsers, and applications. Older browsers like Internet Explorer, some versions of Safari, and many desktop applications (including Microsoft Office, Adobe Photoshop older versions, and various email clients) do not support WebP format. Converting WebP to JPG ensures your images are accessible to every user, regardless of their browser or device.

JPG (or JPEG) remains the most widely supported image format on the internet. It has been the standard for photographic images since the 1990s and is compatible with virtually every web browser, content management system, email client, photo editing software, and social media platform. When you convert WebP to JPG, you sacrifice some of the file size savings of WebP in exchange for universal compatibility. For most use cases — including website content, email marketing, social media posts, and document embedding — JPG is the safer, more reliable choice.

When Should You Convert WebP to JPG?

There are several scenarios where converting WebP images to JPG is recommended. If you are sending images via email, many email clients strip WebP support and will display a broken image icon. If you are uploading images to a CMS or platform that does not support WebP (such as older versions of WordPress or certain ecommerce platforms), you need JPG format. If you are sharing images with clients or colleagues who may not have modern browsers, JPG ensures everyone can view the images. For print materials, JPG is the standard format. Our converter maintains high visual quality during the conversion process, so your images look great in both formats.
