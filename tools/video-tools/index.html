Free Video Tools for Content Creators and Video Editors

Our Video Snippet Adjusters category brings together 6 browser-based tools that help you work with video specifications and calculations. Whether you need to calculate video aspect ratios and find matching dimensions, add and convert video duration timestamps, convert between common video frame rates, calculate upscaled video resolutions while maintaining aspect ratio, estimate video file sizes based on resolution and duration, or generate YouTube video thumbnail URLs from video IDs — these tools simplify your video workflow.

All tools run entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your computer, ensuring complete privacy.

Video Calculation Tools

Calculate aspect ratios for any video format and find matching dimensions. Convert between common frame rates including 24fps, 30fps, and 60fps. Estimate video file sizes based on resolution, duration, and bitrate.

Video Utility Tools

Add, subtract, and convert video duration timestamps. Calculate upscaled resolutions while maintaining proper aspect ratio. Generate YouTube video thumbnail URLs from video IDs for embedding and sharing.

Additional Video Tools

Use the Resolution Upscaler to calculate upscaled video dimensions while keeping the correct aspect ratio for your target display.
