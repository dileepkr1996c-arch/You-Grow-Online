What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text commonly used in graphic design, print, and web publishing to fill space before the final content is ready. Derived from a scrambled passage of Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" written in 45 BC, Lorem Ipsum has been the standard dummy text of the printing and typesetting industry for over 500 years. Unlike regular English text, Lorem Ipsum has a natural-looking distribution of letters and words that closely resembles readable content.

Designers and developers use Lorem Ipsum to visualize how text will look in a layout before the actual content is written. It helps clients focus on the design rather than getting distracted by readable content. Our generator lets you choose between standard Lorem Ipsum and the original Cicero text, and you can generate by paragraphs, sentences, or word count.

Why Use Lorem Ipsum?

Using placeholder text keeps the focus on design elements like typography, spacing, and layout rather than the content itself. It prevents reviewers from getting sidetracked by reading and evaluating the actual text. Lorem Ipsum is also useful for creating wireframes, prototypes, and style guides during the early stages of website and app development.
